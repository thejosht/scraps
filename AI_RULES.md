# Scraps AI Rules

## Current Status

No AI API calls are implemented in this foundation. Local recipe templates and deterministic ranking should come first.

## Future AI Role

AI may later help with wording, substitutions, or expanding a selected template, but it should not replace the decision engine.

## Guardrails

- Scraps is a food decision app, not a generic AI recipe generator.
- Prefer practical meals using the user's actual ingredients.
- Keep suggestions stable for identical inputs.
- Do not invent required ingredients the user did not select.
- Separate required ingredients from optional upgrades.
- Respect appliances, time, effort, cleanup, and safety constraints.
- Keep safety notes direct, especially for poultry, eggs, leftovers, and reheating.

## Output Style Later

Suggestions should be concise, grounded, and useful. Avoid overconfident culinary claims, novelty for novelty's sake, or long recipe essays unless the user asks for details.
