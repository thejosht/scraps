# Scraps PRD

## Summary

Scraps helps users quickly decide what to cook with ingredients they already have. It is a food decision app: fast input, clear constraints, and realistic meal ideas.

## Core Promise

Open Scraps, tap what you have, choose a vibe, and get realistic meal ideas fast.

## Target Users

- Busy home cooks who need dinner direction.
- Budget-conscious users trying to use what is already available.
- Beginners who need realistic, forgiving ideas.
- Repeat users who want a fast answer without a full planning ritual.

## Core Flow

1. Select available ingredients through progressive disclosure.
2. Select appliances or cooking constraints.
3. Choose one or more vibes.
4. Add a situation if helpful, such as low effort, leftovers, breakfast, or late night.
5. Receive a short set of ranked meal ideas with practical notes.

## Product Principles

- Decisions over generation.
- Realistic meals over impressive prose.
- Stable suggestions over random output.
- Responsive-first interactions.
- Premium playful utility: polished, warm, useful, and adult-friendly.

## Non-Goals For Now

- No Supabase.
- No AI API calls.
- No Stripe or payments.
- No full app UI in the foundation task.
